# Privacy Policy for Last Idea

Last updated: May 2026

Last Idea is a local-first Markdown idea catcher.

## What Last Idea Collects

Last Idea does not collect personal data.

The app has:

- No ads
- No analytics
- No tracking SDKs
- No account system
- No server or backend
- No data selling or data sharing

## Where Your Ideas Are Stored

Your ideas are stored as plain Markdown files on your device.

If you choose a folder through Android's system picker, Last Idea writes your
Markdown files to that selected folder using Android's Storage Access Framework.
The app does not provide its own cloud sync service.

## Android Permissions

Last Idea currently requests no Android permissions.

Folder access is handled through Android's system folder picker. You choose the
folder, and Android grants the app access to that folder.

## Password Lock

If you enable password lock, it is local only.

Last Idea does not know, receive, or send your password anywhere. Password
verification happens on your device.
